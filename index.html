Datenschutzerklärung

Was die App tut

  • Pursonal zeigt deine Bildschirmzeit des Tages und die Stimmung eines Tieres. Dafür nutzt die App Apples Schnittstellen Family Controls und Device Activity, mit deiner Zustimmung.
  • Welche Apps und Kategorien du auswählst, speichert iOS als geschützte Marken (Tokens). Die App sieht keine App-Namen und keinen Verlauf, sondern nur Schwellen der Gesamtzeit (zum Beispiel „1 Minute erreicht“).
  • Die Zahlen der Schwellen liegen in einem gemeinsamen Speicher von App und Erweiterung auf deinem Gerät (App Group) und verlassen das Gerät nicht. Die daraus gebildeten Tageswerte der Historie werden zusätzlich in deiner privaten iCloud gespeichert (siehe Tabelle).

Privacy policy

What the app does

  • Pursonal shows your screen time for the day and the mood of an animal. For this, the app uses Apple’s Family Controls and Device Activity frameworks, with your permission.
  • The apps and categories you choose are stored by iOS as protected tokens. The app does not see app names or any history, only thresholds of your total time (for example “1 minute reached”).
  • The threshold numbers are kept in storage shared by the app and its extension on your device (App Group) and never leave the device. The daily values for the history that are derived from them are additionally stored in your private iCloud (see table).

  • There is no server of our own. The iCloud data belongs to your Apple account; the provider cannot read it.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling and no sharing of data with third parties. Apple processes iCloud data and the purchase as a separate service under its own terms.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

Notifications

  • Optional. The app sends a notification locally on your device when the animal’s mood gets worse. No data is sent to a server.

Your rights

  • Turn off screen time access at any time: iPhone Settings › Screen Time.
  • Delete data: deleting the app removes the local data. iCloud data: iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Pursonal.
  • Questions and access requests: by e-mail to ZenSwan@proton.me.
  • You can withdraw your consents (screen time, notifications) at any time in the iPhone settings. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); for the purchase it is the contract via the App Store.
  • You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority; in North Rhine-Westphalia that is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
